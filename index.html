About

As an Australian based sourcing company, we save you money and time taking the stress out of product sourcing and importing from Asia, Europe and the Pacific Islands.

As a business in Australia, it can be challenging to find the right manufacturing partner. when you partner with Valence and Co, we do the hard work for you. Our 20+ years of experience and expansive supplier network ensure you’re not only dealing with the very best of factories but that you receive quality products that meet your specifications and requirements.

Benefits of sourcing with Valence and Co
01 / LOW MINIMUM ORDER QUANTITIES

Low minimum order quantities

Are you tired of getting turned away by manufacturers with minimum order quantities of 1000 or more when you only need 100? At Valence and Co we work with you to find the right solution for your business. In most cases, if you purchase from our core range of products, we can consolidate orders allowing for smaller order quantities and a workable solution for you.

02 / FREIGHT & CLEARANCE

Remove the hassle of dealing with freight forwarders

As a full-service sourcing company, we can take care of all the freight, clearance, importing and delivery management. Your product will be delivered to you without you having to take care of all the tedious and complicated administration. We work with several freight companies, allowing our import team to select the best option taking cost and timing into consideration.

03 / TOTAL SOLUTION

Total product sourcing solution

Importing products can be a complicated and frustrating learning curve. Save time, hassle and money with our total product sourcing solution. As a professional sourcing company, we do all the leg work ensuring your products are imported hassle free, in your required packaging and branding without the risks you would face going it alone.

04 / VETTED FACILITIES

Vetted manufacturing facilities

Selecting the wrong manufacturer can end in disaster and end up costing you more. Reduce the risk of doing business with a manufacturer you don’t know and save the many hours you would spend trawling the internet. As an Australian sourcing company, we pride ourselves on our expertise, ensuring all manufacturers meet our strict requirements so that your product is delivered on time and to specification.

05 / COST MINIMALIZATION

Cost minimalization

Our wide-reaching and comprehensive client and supplier network allow us to consolidate orders where possible, allowing our clients to order lower quantities at a cheaper cost per unit. We also have the capacity to combine multiple orders into one container, saving on freight and allowing cost distribution, reducing your overall outlay.

06 / ONLINE ORDER TRACKING

Online order tracking

All our clients are given access to our leading online order tracking portal. Keep up to date with the exact location of your goods, from the port to custom clearance, with the click of a button. Plus, every time your order status is updated, you will receive an email notification notifying you of the change, ensuring you are constantly kept in the loop.

Our product sourcing process

End-to-end sourcing.
From enquiry to delivery.

As a dedicated sourcing company, our end-to-end product sourcing and import process allows us to source the right product you need, from the right supplier.

01

Enquiry / Analysis

From the outset, we take the time to get to know you and your business to understand your budget and requirements. Asking the right questions enables our team to ensure we understand your product sourcing goals..

02

Research

Once we have a clear picture of what you require, we will tap into our diverse manufacturer network in order to find the factories that match your needs and product requests. We place importance on quality, assurance, and compliance.

03

Quote

Once a suitable manufacturer has been chosen, our team will put together a detailed formal quote for your consideration. This includes all information from order quantity, to cost per unit, delivery details/ charges and estimated delivery date.

04

Sampling

As an experienced sourcing company, we keep on hand, samples of our core product range, enabling us to provide samples for your viewing. For custom products, we can order samples direct from the manufacturer for your approval before production begins.

05

Packaging & Design

We deliver a full-service solution, offering both packaging and design services, alongside the sourcing component. Our team of graphic designers will work with your existing brand guidelines to create a unified look and feel.

06

Payment

Generally a deposit invoice will be issued once all product samples, packaging and design have been approved. Upon receipt, manufacturing will begin. Final payment is due on shipment.

07

Production

Our product sourcing team handles the entire process from start to finish. We liaise regularly with the manufacturer to ensure timely production schedules, whilst also carrying out quality control measures to ensure a high-quality end result.

08

Shipping & Logistics

All goods undergo a final inspection and quality check before shipping. We then take care of all freight, clearance, importation and delivery of all goods to your door. Our end-to-end process allows you to grow your business without the hassle of importing.

FAQ

[ Selected clients & partners ]
01

I want to import products, but I don’t know where to start?

Take the stress out of importing and let us take care of your product sourcing and import needs. At Valence and Co we take care of sourcing, manufacturing, freight and delivery direct to your door. Import goods with an experienced sourcing partner without the hassle of having to do it yourself. Our team is happy to organise product samples or simply have a chat.

02

What about quality, how do you guarantee this with imported products?

We monitor the entire process from start to finish, requesting samples for approval to ensure a high-quality end result. Manufacturing won’t begin until product sign-off and all goods undergo strict quality control and final production sample approval before shipping. We also ensure products meet Australian regulations and standards where applicable.

03

How do you negotiate the best price?

Our long-standing relationships enable us to negotiate better prices for our clients. We also have the ability to consolidate smaller orders where possible to take advantage of economies of scale, not only reducing cost per unit, but also allowing smaller order quantities.

04

I don’t understand freight or insurance?

Don’t worry, you don’t need to. As part of our service, we take care of freight, insurance and delivery; everything is included in our cost. As sourcing and import specialists, we are able to negotiate competitive prices with international freight companies, customs and insurance brokers.

05

How long does it take for my goods to arrive?

This is very dependent on the individual product, the quantity ordered, and the type of freight used. At the outset, you will be provided with a complete timeline with estimated delivery time frames.

06

How can I find out where my shipment is?

We offer clients access to our industry leading online tracking portal. Once your quote has been approved you will receive your unique portal logins. From here you can easily check the status and exact location of your goods. You will also receive email notifications each time your order status is updated.

07

Doesn’t importing require a special import licence?

In some cases, certain products can require specific Australian licences and approvals. In these situations, our licenced customs officer will work with you to ensure you have all the information you need. Valence and Co holds all relevant licenses to import a wide range of products including dangerous goods.

08

What restrictions are there on importing goods from China?

As product sourcing specialists, we are well versed in the relevant importation requirements, regulations and restrictions. If there are particular product quarantine requirements or customs regulations, we will inform you.

09

Do you only work with certain industries?

We work with a vast array of businesses and industries. Our experience and expertise are wide reaching, enabling us to handle all your manufacturing, sourcing and importation needs so that you can focus your attention where it’s needed.

10

Will my order be big enough?

In most cases we can accommodate small orders inc. custom labelling. Our long standing relationships with manufactures allows our team to process small MOQ orders with private labelling. If you have a product you’d like to find out about, reach out to our team and we’ll see what we can do.
